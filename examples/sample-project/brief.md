## Script (45s, ~135 words at 170 WPM)

### Hook (0:00 - 0:03)
"Your website is invisible to AI."

### Setup (0:03 - 0:15)
"Right now, millions of people are searching with ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini instead of Google. And when they ask 'who's the best provider in my area,' your website either shows up as the recommended answer... or it doesn't exist."

### Payoff (0:15 - 0:38)
"AI search traffic converts 4.4 times higher than traditional search. That's not a typo. These are buyers who already trust the recommendation. But AI engines don't crawl your site like Google does. They need structured data, clear expertise signals, and content that directly answers questions. The businesses getting recommended right now built their sites for this moment."

### CTA (0:38 - 0:45)
"If you want to see exactly where your business stands in AI search, link in bio to book your free strategy call."
